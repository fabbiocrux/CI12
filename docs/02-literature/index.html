Mental model when reading an abstract

  1. 🌍 Topic → the writer provides background factual information.
  2. ⚠️ Problem → The writer presents the gap/problem to treat
  3. 🔎 Research Gap → the writer states the limits of current knowledge.
  4. 🎯 The purpose : the writer presents the general aim and the specific aim of the study.
  5. 📜 Methodology → the writer summarises the methodology and provides details.
  6. 📊 Results → the writer indicates the achievement of the study.
  7. 🚀 Perspectives → the writer presents the implications of the study.

In Science …

We don’t sell products!! .. We sell Gaps!

Source: Miles, D.A., 2017. A Taxonomy of Research Gaps: Identifying and Defining the Seven Research Gaps.

Evidence Gaps

  • An evidence gap occurs with a provocative exception arises if a new research finding contradicts widely accepted conclusions.

  • If results from studies allow for conclusions in their own right, but are contradictory when examined from a more abstract point of view.

Knowledge Gaps

  • Most common gap.
  • knowledge may not exist in the actual field to theories and literature from related research domains.
  • it might be the case that results of a study differs from what was expected

Practical-Knowledge Gaps

  • Tends to be a discrepancy that can motivate new research in this direction.

  • A practical–knowledge (action-knowledge) conflict arises when the actual behavior of professionals is different from their advocated behavior.

  • Research could seek to determine the scope of the conflict and to uncover the reasons for its existence

Methodological Gap

  • It deals with the conflict that occurs due to the influence of methodology on research results..

  • This gap addresses the conflicts with the research methods in the prior studies and offers a new line of research that is divergent from those research methods.

  • It might be useful to vary research methods, especially if certain research topics have been mainly explored using a singular or common method

Empirical Gap

  • It deals with gaps in the prior research.

  • This conflict deals with the research findings or propositions need to be evaluated or empirically verified.

  • The empirical gap often addresses conflicts that no study to date has directly attempted to evaluate a subject or topic from an empirical approach

Theoretical Gap

  • It deals with the gaps in theory with the prior research.

  • If one phenomenon is being explained through various theoretical models, similar to a methodological gap conflict, there might be a theoretical conflict.

  • Researchers and scholars could examine whether one of those theories is superior in terms of the gap in the prior research.

  • Theoretical gaps are a common occurrence in examining prior research on a phenomenon

Population Gap

  • There are always underserved populations that have been under-researched.

  • The type of research regarding the population that is not adequately represented or under-researched in the evidence base or prior research (e.g., gender, race/ethnicity, age and etic)
