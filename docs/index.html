Goal of the module:

This introduction course seeks to to Master and Engineering students to the basis of the scientific research in Innovation Management field. The main purpose is to understand the process of establishing a research process pipeline, including the literature review, structuration of the research problematic, definition of experimental protocol and hypothesis and presentation of the results in from of a scientific paper.
